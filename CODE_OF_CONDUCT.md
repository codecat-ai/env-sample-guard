# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free
experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Public or private abuse.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful to the project community.

Instances can be reported through the repository's normal maintainer contact
channels. Reports will be reviewed with care and confidentiality.
